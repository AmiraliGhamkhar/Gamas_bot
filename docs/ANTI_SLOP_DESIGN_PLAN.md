# Gamas Mini App — Anti-Slop Design Plan

> **Status: implemented.** Phases 0–5 are applied in `miniapp/` (see
> `miniapp/DESIGN.md` for the resulting design law and
> `src/test/design_lint.test.ts` for the enforced guardrails). Phase 6 is the
> ongoing checklist.

A design audit of `miniapp/` and a phased plan to make it look like **Gamas** and
not like *an app*. Written from a mini-app designer's desk, against the code as
it stands on this branch.

The governing idea, borrowed verbatim from the booklet engine
(`docs/BOOKLET_DESIGN.md`) because it is the right idea:

> Content is generated by the AI; design is controlled by the Gamas engine.

The booklet side obeys it. The mini app does not — it currently looks like the
default output of "dark Tailwind dashboard with amber accent", which is exactly
what "AI slop" means in 2026: competent, anonymous, and interchangeable.

---

## 1. What "AI slop" is (and is not) here

Slop is **not** bad craft. The mini app's craft is genuinely good and most of it
must be protected:

- Persian numerals everywhere, Jalali dates, tabular figures (`lib/format.ts`).
- A hand-drawn, single-weight, 26-icon inline set (`ui/Icon.tsx`) — no icon font,
  no emoji.
- One ink scale + one accent, no rainbow (`tailwind.config.js`).
- Calm, specific Persian copy ("سؤال‌های کوتاه برای مرور سریع در زمان‌های مرده").
- Real accessibility: focus-visible, aria roles, 44 px targets, global
  `prefers-reduced-motion` (`index.css`).
- Haptics used as punctuation, not confetti (`lib/telegram.ts`).

Slop is the **visual language** around that craft: the look that survives if you
swap the logo and the strings and nothing changes. Six tells, all present:

### T1 — The anonymous dark template
`#0B0C0E` background, `#F0B54A` amber accent, everything on it. The app hardcodes
dark: `setHeaderColor("#0B0C0E")`, `color-scheme: dark`, `theme-color #0B0C0E`,
and `colorScheme` is declared in `lib/telegram.ts` but never read. A dark
mini-app that ignores Telegram's theme is the single loudest generated-by-default
signal. It also clashes with the product's own brand, which is navy/teal/cream
(see T6).

### T2 — Card soup
Everything is the same `rounded-card border border-line bg-surface` card:
identity card, balance card, plans card (inside a section), reward card, payment
card, library rows, review rows, empty states, processing states. Nested cards
(`AccountTab` plan picker is a `Card` of bordered buttons), card-with-divide-y
rows, cards used as buttons. When every surface has identical weight, hierarchy
exists only in font size — the "bento of interchangeable boxes" look.

### T3 — The circle-badge icon cliché
`flex h-N w-N rounded-full bg-surface2` with a thin-line icon inside appears in
`EmptyState`, `Stat`, `PackRow`, `AssetRow`, the avatar fallback, the balance
card, and the banned screen. This motif is the visual equivalent of the word
"seamlessly".

### T4 — The template state/motion kit
Shimmer skeleton → `animate-rise` stagger → `animate-pop` on rewards → tinted
ok/soft-bad result blocks. Fine individually; as a set it is the standard
component-library kit. One more tell: `LibraryTab` shows
`<Progress value={1} total={3} />` for "processing" — fake determinate progress.

### T5 — Wallet-first information architecture
Landing tab is **حساب من**, whose seven sections are identity, balance,
subscription, referrals, rewards catalog, payment history, support. The first
screen of a *study* product reads like a fintech dashboard, and the only
brand-colored button on it is "خرید در ربات". Account is a kitchen sink; money
and study are given equal surface.

### T6 — Zero domain DNA in-app (the deepest tell)
The brand already has a complete identity — and the app shares none of it:

| Brand owns (booklet system) | App uses |
| --- | --- |
| Navy `#04202C…#0B4658`, teal `#087F8C…#3FB6C0`, cyan, cream `#FFF9ED` paper | Neutral gray ink + amber |
| ECG trace rule as a signature motif | nothing |
| Open-book monogram (`assets/booklet/brand/monogram.png`) | a lone "گ" glyph in the splash |
| Topic heroes and emblems (medical, pharma, anatomy, nursing…) | nothing |
| Navy banner = "major destination" register | nothing |

Run the **"could be any product?" test** on any screenshot: replace "گاماس" with
"a crypto wallet" and nothing breaks. That is the slop.

Minor tells, cheap to fix: `backdrop-blur` glass on the nav; the loanword
**«اکسپلور»** in an otherwise Persian UI; identical empty-state anatomy
(circle + title + two lines + button) across all screens.

## 2. Strategy: reconnect, don't redesign

Anti-sloping is not adding polish — it is making the interface *specific*.
Gamas already paid for specificity on the document side. The plan is therefore
mostly **recombination and subtraction**:

1. **Move the app onto the brand palette** (navy ink, teal/cyan accent, cream
   reserved for study content).
2. **Break card soup** with a small set of surface registers and archetypes.
3. **Put the domain in the room**: ECG rule, monogram, topic emblems — as
   structure, not wallpaper.
4. **Reorder the product around studying**, push money into a sheet.
5. **Give motion and loading one signature** instead of the generic kit.
6. **Write the rules down** so the next feature cannot re-slop the app.

Target feel: **a study desk at night** — navy ink chrome, warm paper cards for
questions and notes, a teal ECG line as the brand's pulse. Nobody else's
mini-app looks like that; every generic dashboard does look like what we have.

## 3. The plan

### Phase 0 — Guardrails (½ day, do first)
- Add `miniapp/DESIGN.md`: tokens, archetypes, the reserved-red rule
  (red = warning/error only, straight from `BOOKLET_DESIGN.md`), and the
  checklist in §4.
- Mirror the booklet enforcement test: a vitest that fails on a raw `#RRGGBB`
  literal inside `src/**/*.tsx` (styles live in `tailwind.config.js` only).
  `tests/test_booklet_design.py` proves this works.

### Phase 1 — Token surgery (½–1 day)
Rename/retune the theme to brand semantics in `tailwind.config.js`:

| Today | Becomes | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bg #0B0C0E` | `navy-950`-tinted ink (`#04202C`→ darkened) | booklet `Palette` |
| `surface/2/3` grays | navy-900/800/700 steps | " |
| `brand #F0B54A` amber | `teal-500 #3FB6C0` (accent), `cyan-300` for highlights | " |
| `brandInk/brandSoft` | `tealInk/tealSoft` derived | " |
| `ok/bad/soft` | keep semantics; recolor to `success/danger` of the booklet scale | " |
| *(new)* `paper #FFF9ED` | **study-content surfaces only** | " |
| *(new)* `ecg` | hairline rule token | `decorative/ecg-line` |

Also in this phase:
- `lib/telegram.ts`: read `colorScheme`/`themeParams` and at least stop fighting
  the host (header/background follow our navy; light theme is Phase 1b if wanted
  — not blocking).
- Splash: use the real `brand/monogram.png` (or redraw it as SVG) with the ECG
  line animating once under it — instead of the "گ" square.
- Desktop/web (`max-w-md` column in a void): tint the void with `navy-950` and
  drop a very low-contrast monogram watermark at `>640px`.

### Phase 2 — Kill card soup (1–2 days)
Define **three surface registers** and **four archetypes**, then re-cut every
screen:

- **Chrome** (nav, headers, toolbars): flat navy, hairline borders, no cards.
- **Paper** (things worth reading: question cards, explanations, booklet-like
  content): cream `paper` surface, dark ink text, a 2 px teal ECG rule on top,
  the one shadow in the app. This is the app's moment of delight — a review
  question should feel like an index card on the desk.
- **Banner** (one per screen max): the navy banner register from the booklets —
  "a major destination". Use it for the balance on Account and the round header
  on Explore.

Archetypes replacing the generic card:
1. **Object row** (pack, asset, payment): flat list rows on hairline dividers —
   `LibraryTab`'s `divide-y` payment list is already the model; extend it to
   packs and assets and delete the row-cards.
2. **Paper card** (question, explanation, empty-state illustration).
3. **Banner** (above).
4. **Grouped list** (settings-like sections on Account): section label + flat
   group with dividers, iOS-settings rhythm — not seven floating cards.

`EmptyState` loses the circle badge: it takes an **emblem** prop drawn from
`assets/booklet/icons/` (book, capsule, bone, flask, chart…) chosen per screen —
the topic-aware trick the booklet covers already use (`assets.py` +
`manifest.json`). Copy stays per-screen (it already is — keep it).

### Phase 3 — Study-first IA (1 day + 2 product calls)
- **Tab order** → `مرور · کتابخانه · اکسپلور · حساب من` (Review home; RTL
  rightmost = most used). Landing on a wallet is T5; landing on "your questions"
  is the product.
- **Split Account**: identity + credit banner + "ادامهٔ مرور"/"بسته‌های من" on
  the tab; subscription, rewards catalog, payment history and referral stats
  move into a **«کیف پول» bottom sheet** opened from the balance banner. The
  deep links ("خرید در ربات") are unchanged.
- **Naming**: «اکسپلور» → «یک‌سؤال» (or «تمرین») — an all-Persian tab bar;
  "پلن" → «بسته» in plan labels. *Note:* `docs/DEPLOY_MINIAPP.md` §6 and the tab
  smoke checklist pin the four labels — update them in the same change.
- Delete the fake `<Progress value={1} total={3} />`; processing shows honest
  staged text ("در حال بازکردن صوت → رونویسی → جزوه") which the pipeline already
  knows.

### Phase 4 — One signature of motion (½–1 day)
- **Loading**: replace shimmer bars with the brand's pulse — a teal ECG hairline
  that sweeps once per cycle (CSS stroke animation over `ecg-line`), keep the
  card-rhythm skeletons for lists.
- **Answer feedback**: paper card tints only its ECG rule (teal = درست,
  red = نادرست — reserved-red respected) plus the existing verdict block;
  drop the `animate-pop` coin-jingle on `+امتیاز` for a quiet teal increment.
- Keep `rise` stagger (capped at 6 — already good), keep all of it behind
  `prefers-reduced-motion` (already global). No animation libraries.

### Phase 5 — Voice pass (½ day)
Most strings are already human — audit only for the residual template register:
- "پلن ۲۴ ساعته" → «بستهٔ ۲۴ ساعته»; "کاربر گاماس" fallback → the user's first
  name or «دانش‌آموز گاماس»; any string that could appear on another product's
  screen gets rewritten with a study-specific noun.
- Statuses obey the reserved-red rule everywhere (payment "ردشده" may be red;
  "لغوشده" stays neutral — already true, keep it).

### Phase 6 — Keep it unslopped (ongoing)
- Every new screen must pass three gates: the "could be any product?" test, the
  checklist in §4, and "does it use an archetype?".
- Visual snapshot of the four tabs (vitest + jsdom can't screenshot; a tiny
  Playwright script or a phone photo ritual is enough) reviewed per release.

## 4. The anti-slop checklist (post on the wall)

Never in this app:
- [ ] Gradients (except the one ECG stroke), glassmorphism, glow shadows
- [ ] Emoji as UI (icons are the `Icon.tsx` vocabulary or booklet emblems)
- [ ] A third accent color; amber `#F0B54A` leaves the app entirely
- [ ] Cards nested in cards; cards used as buttons; a card with only one child
- [ ] Circle-badge + generic icon as an illustration substitute
- [ ] Determinate progress that is actually indeterminate
- [ ] Streaks, confetti, badges, leaderboards, "شگفت‌انگیز!" copy
- [ ] Marketing hero language inside the product ("پتانسیل خود را آزاد کنید")
- [ ] A screen that would survive a logo swap ("could be any product?" test)
- [ ] A raw `#RRGGBB` in a component (linted, Phase 0)

Always in this app:
- [ ] Persian digits, Jalali dates, tabular figures (format.ts is law)
- [ ] All-Persian UI naming; Latin only for real Latin nouns (PDF, Word)
- [ ] One paper surface per screen at most; one banner per screen at most
- [ ] 44 px targets, focus-visible, reduced-motion (already enforced)
- [ ] Copy that names the study object ("بسته", "سؤال", "جزوه", "مرور")

## 5. Order, effort, risk

| Phase | Effort | Touches | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 Guardrails | ½ d | tests, DESIGN.md | none |
| 1 Tokens + splash | 1 d | tailwind.config.js, index.css, telegram.ts, App.tsx | low (visual only) |
| 2 Archetypes | 1–2 d | all four tabs, primitives.tsx, states.tsx | medium — biggest diff |
| 3 IA + naming | 1 d | App.tsx, AccountTab, labels, DEPLOY_MINIAPP.md | product decision needed (tab order, «اکسپلور» rename) |
| 4 Motion | ½–1 d | index.css, Explore/Review feedback | low |
| 5 Voice | ½ d | strings across tabs | low |

≈ **4–6 focused days** end to end. Phase 2 is the one that changes what the app
*is*; Phases 1 and 3 are what make it *Gamas*.

Deliberately out of scope: a light theme following Telegram's `colorScheme`
(worth doing later, not required to de-slop), new illustration commissions (the
`assets/booklet` set is enough), the bot's chat-side UI (its own audit), and any
redesign of the review-sheet HTML (it already follows the booklet system).
