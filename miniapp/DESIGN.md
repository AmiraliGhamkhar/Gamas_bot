# Gamas Mini App — design law

The Mini App obeys the same governing principle as the booklet engine
(`docs/BOOKLET_DESIGN.md`):

> Content is generated by the AI; design is controlled by the Gamas engine.

Concretely: no component chooses a colour, invents a surface or coins a
metaphor. Everything below is decided here; `src/test/design_lint.test.ts`
fails the build when a component drifts.

## The identity

The Gamas identity lives in `gamas_bot/document/theme.py` (`Palette`) — deep
navy structure, teal/cyan accents, warm cream paper, ink-black text, and
**red reserved** for warnings, failures and critical distinctions (a wrong
answer is a critical distinction; a cancelation is not). The Mini App theme
(`tailwind.config.js`) is that palette adapted for a dark, phone-first chrome:

| Register | Tokens | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chrome** | `bg`, `surface`, `surface2`, `surface3`, `line`, `line2`, `text`, `muted`, `faint` | Nav, headers, rows, sheets — flat navy |
| **Accent** | `brand`, `brandInk`, `brandSoft` | Teal actions: buttons, active tab, focus |
| **Paper** | `paper`, `paper2`, `ink`, `inkMuted`, `trace` | Study content only: question cards |
| **Banner** | `banner` (booklet navy_700) | One navy banner per screen at most |
| **Status** | `ok/okSoft/okInk`, `bad/badSoft/badInk` | Semantics only; red is reserved |

Two typefaces are never mixed; digits are always Persian (`lib/format.ts` is
the single source — no component re-implements it).

## Surface registers and archetypes

Three registers, four archetypes. A new screen composes these; it does not
invent a fifth.

1. **Object row** — a flat `Row` in a `Group` on hairline dividers. Packs,
   assets, payments, settings. Never a card per row.
2. **Paper card** — `PaperCard`: cream, ink text, one shadow, and the ECG rule
   along the top (`ui/Ecg.tsx`, the brand's signature motif). Things worth
   reading live on paper: questions, explanations. The rule carries the
   verdict — teal while unanswered, `okInk`/`badInk` after.
3. **Banner** — `Banner`: the booklet's navy banner register. The balance on
   حساب من. One per screen, ever.
4. **Grouped list** — `Group`: section label + rows. Settings-like sections.

The tab bar is study-first: **مرور · کتابخانه · یک‌سؤال · حساب من**. Money
(اشتراک، امتیاز، پرداخت‌ها، دعوت) lives behind the «کیف پول» `Sheet`, never
on a tab.

## Motion

One signature: the ECG pulse. It is the loading indicator (`EcgLoader`) and
the paper rule; list skeletons keep the card rhythm. Everything else is a
2% press (`pressable`) or a capped rise. All motion respects
`prefers-reduced-motion` (global, `index.css`).

## Voice

All-Persian UI naming — no social-app loanwords («اکسپلور» died for this).
Copy names the study object (بسته، سؤال، جزوه، مرور) and sounds like a good
teaching assistant, not a growth team. Units are spoken as a student would
("۱ ساعت و ۳۰ دقیقه").

## The anti-slop checklist

Never in this app:

- [ ] Raw `#RRGGBB` in a component (linted — the theme owns colour)
- [ ] Gradients, glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur`), glow shadows (linted)
- [ ] A third accent colour; amber is not ours
- [ ] Cards nested in cards; cards used as buttons; a card with one child
- [ ] Circle-badge + generic icon standing in for an illustration —
      empty states use booklet emblems (`ui/Emblem.tsx`)
- [ ] Determinate progress that is actually indeterminate
- [ ] Streaks, confetti, badges, leaderboards, hype copy
- [ ] A screen that would survive a logo swap — the "could be any product?" test

Always in this app:

- [ ] Persian digits, Jalali dates, tabular figures (`lib/format.ts` is law)
- [ ] Paper for study content, chrome for everything else
- [ ] One paper card and one banner per screen at most
- [ ] 44 px targets, focus-visible, reduced-motion (enforced in `index.css`)
- [ ] Status colours obey the reserved-red rule
